Edward Huang

Founder and Director, IGWT Institute for Games, Written-narrative & Tropes

University of Toronto

Edward Huang is an undergraduate student in Sociology and East Asian Studies at the University of Toronto (St. George), where he is completing his Bachelor of Arts (2023–2027), and was an international exchange student in the School of Humanities and Social Sciences at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2025–2026). In 2026, he founded the IGWT Institute for Games, Written-narrative & Tropes, a Canadian not-for-profit research institute and publisher based in Toronto.

His work focuses on long-form criticism and research on digital games, visual novels, interactive narrative, animation, and internet visual culture. Alongside his independent criticism, he has contributed to user and market research, content editing, marketing communications, and character design at Tencent, NetEase Youdao, and ChillyRoom. He is a native speaker of Mandarin, with professional working proficiency in English and conversational proficiency in Cantonese.

Research interests

  • Games as an art form and narrative design
  • Visual novels and branching written narrative
  • Interactive narrative, player agency, and metagaming
  • Animation and East Asian popular media
  • Internet visual culture and character tropes
  • Player research and media sociology
